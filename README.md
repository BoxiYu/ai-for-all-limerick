# AI for All · A Winter's Night in Limerick

An immersive 3D talk for **AI for All** at Limerick City Library (Thursday 26 November 2026), by Dr Boxi Yu, Lero, University of Limerick.

Open it: **https://boxiyu.github.io/ai-for-all-limerick/**

One winter evening in Limerick, eight lit windows. Inside each, a person and AI have a short conversation: you ask, AI looks or listens, AI answers, you decide. Then a look behind the windows: the phone, the network, a data centre, the chips and the power that bring AI into everyday life.

Keys: → next · ← back · 1–8 a window · J the journey · V narration · C captions · F full screen.

The pictures and the narrator's voice were made with AI (Nano Banana Pro and Gemini). The conversations are prepared examples; nothing calls a live AI model. Built with three.js.
